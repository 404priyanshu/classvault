## Intent

Create a short ClassVault launch promo using the live marketing site and the repository's approved clubhouse art and visual identity. Show three implemented actions: finding and previewing notes, building source-cited study roadmaps, and joining realtime study rooms with a shared Pomodoro timer and chat. End with the actual early-access positioning and a single sign-up call to action.

## Assets

- Live public site capture: `capture/`, taken from `https://www.classvault.in` on 2026-09-23.
- Approved local artwork: `assets/study-clubhouse.webp`, `assets/spot-note.webp`, `assets/spot-roadmap.webp`, and `assets/spot-pomodoro.webp`.
- The vault mark is the SVG path in `src/components/landing/VaultMark.tsx`.

## Customizations

- 1920 × 1080, 30 fps, readable without sound.
- Use the current periwinkle, butter yellow, mint, and deep ink palette with Inter typography.
- Use a quiet original instrumental bed; no voiceover or account-dependent media service.
- Do not imply video/audio calling in study rooms, automatic campus verification for every student, or universal campus availability.

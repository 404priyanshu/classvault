06 / Common questions

Before you open the vault.

How does campus verification work?

Choose your university during onboarding. When your confirmed email matches an approved academic domain, ClassVault marks that membership verified; otherwise it stays pending.

What makes note ratings trustworthy?

Ranking uses rating volume and recency alongside average score, so a note with one perfect rating cannot automatically outrank material trusted over time.

Can someone see a roadmap source they cannot access?

No. Each view rechecks source permission. If a source is unavailable, the section derived from it is withheld instead of leaking its contents.

What happens when a study-room host leaves?

The room can transfer host responsibility to an eligible member, so the shared timer and session do not disappear with the person who started it.

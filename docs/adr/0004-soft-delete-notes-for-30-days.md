# Soft Delete Notes for 30 Days

When a student deletes an eligible uploaded note, ClassVault immediately hides it from visible surfaces and makes it unavailable for study roadmaps, but keeps it restorable by the original uploader from Recently Deleted for 30 days with ratings and rating history preserved. Recently deleted notes still count against upload and storage limits during the recovery window; restoration of a university note requires the original uploader to still have active membership in that university, so a former uploader who switches universities cannot restore it and other university members cannot restore someone else's deleted note. After the recovery window expires, the note's record, file, ratings, and derived study-roadmap data are permanently removed, while platform administrators can force permanent deletion at any time for moderation, legal, abuse, or policy reasons.

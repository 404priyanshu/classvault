# Preserve Uploads After Plan Downgrade

For the MVP, when a Pro student downgrades to the Free tier while exceeding the configured free upload or storage limits, ClassVault preserves all existing notes and keeps them accessible according to normal scope rules. Recently deleted notes still count against storage during their recovery window, and the downgraded student cannot upload new notes until enough content is permanently removed or they upgrade again, trading strict quota enforcement for student data retention and predictable billing transitions.

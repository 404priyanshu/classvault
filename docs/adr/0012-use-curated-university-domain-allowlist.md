# Use Curated University Domain Allowlist

For the MVP, ClassVault verifies university membership through a platform-managed allowlist that maps approved college email domains to university institutions, rather than relying on suffix-only checks such as `.edu` or `.ac.in`. Students can submit university access requests for missing institutions or domains, but only platform administrators can approve domains and create verified university access, trading self-service speed for trust, accuracy, and cleaner private community boundaries.

# Preserve Content After Student Account Deletion

For the MVP, when a platform administrator permanently deletes a student account, ClassVault removes the personal account/profile data but preserves the student's existing notes, university conversations, replies, ratings, saved roadmaps, and shared roadmap links according to their normal scope and access rules. Preserved notes can still receive new ratings, while preserved deleted-user conversation posts remain visible but closed to new replies, trading full author erasure for continuity of community knowledge and stable shared artifacts.
